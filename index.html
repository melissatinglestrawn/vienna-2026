Arriving at VIE

We land at 9:45 AM on Tuesday. Take the CAT (City Airport Train) to Wien Mitte, the Railjet, or a taxi to the apartment. Wien Mitte is also in the 3rd district.

Bags may not transfer automatically between two airlines on this kind of ticket. Ask at check in.

Before we fly

Check that passports are valid well beyond the trip dates. Arrive early at ATL and keep essentials in your carry on.

Getting around Vienna

One pass covers the U-Bahn, trams, buses and S-Bahn inside the city. We ride several times a day, so a pass beats single tickets at about 3.20 euro a ride.

The 7 day Vienna ticket runs about 28.90 euro and covers our whole stay. The 72 hour ticket is the other option. Buy online, in the WienMobil app, or at any station machine.

Buy a Wiener Linien pass

Validate a paper ticket once before your first ride. City passes do not cover the airport run, so buy a separate ticket for that.
