Café Central

Sat, Nov 28, Afternoon

The grand coffee house where Freud, Trotsky and poets once argued.

Visit website

The story

Café Central opened in 1876 inside Palais Ferstel, a palace built for the national bank and stock exchange. Its vaulted, pillared hall looks more like a cathedral than a cafe.

The regulars

Around 1900 it was the living room of Vienna's thinkers. Leon Trotsky played chess here, Sigmund Freud stopped in, and the poet Peter Altenberg used it as his mailing address. A life size figure of Altenberg still sits near the entrance.

Getting there

U3 to Herrengasse, then a 3 minute walk, about 20 minutes

From the apartment on Louise-Martini-Weg. Kardinal-Nagl-Platz (U3) is about 6 minutes on foot.

Good to know

  • Expect a line in the afternoon. It moves steadily.
  • Order a Melange and an Apfelstrudel or a slice of the house cake.
